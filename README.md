## 🔎 Problem-Solving Framework

When I receive a dataset, I first understand the business decision it should support. I then validate the rows, columns, granularity, metrics, and dimensions before checking missing values, duplicates, invalid values, data types, outliers, and inconsistent categories.

Next, I explore trends, relationships, differences, and anomalies. I break the results down by time, category, region, customer, product, and segment, then translate the findings into business language and evidence-based recommendations.
