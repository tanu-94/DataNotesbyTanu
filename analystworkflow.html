How I solve problems as a data analyst

Tools change from project to project. This process doesn't — it's the part that actually produces a trustworthy answer.

01

Problem framing

Define the real business question before touching a dataset.

02

Data collection

Gather every relevant table or file, and confirm nothing needed is missing.

03

Data cleaning

Fix types, nulls, and duplicates so the numbers can be trusted.

04

Exploratory analysis

Dive through where, who, when, and how much — evidence before conclusions.

05

Data modeling

Structure relationships between tables so the report can scale past one question.

06

Dashboard creation

Build the report so a stakeholder can self-serve without needing me in the room.

07

Insights

Trace the finding to its root cause, not just its symptom.

08

Recommendation

Land on a plain-language action the client can actually take.

Example: healthcare staffing project

Business problem — is the hospital short-staffed, and where?
↓
Data audit — validate shift, staff, and absence records
↓
Staff gap analysis — required vs. actual staff, by shift and department
↓
Root cause detection — testing whether overtime and absenteeism explain the gap
↓
Dashboard — a 4-page Power BI report, built for self-serve reading
↓
Recommendation — where coverage should change, and why
