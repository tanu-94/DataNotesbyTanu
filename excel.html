Excel for business reporting

Before a dataset is ready for SQL or Power BI, Excel is often where the first pass of structure and validation happens.

What I use it for

Data preparation

Cleaning raw exports before they're modeled anywhere else.

Pivot tables

Fast first-pass summaries to sanity-check a dataset's shape.

Lookups

VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP to join reference data without a database.

Dashboards

Lightweight reporting views for teams without BI tool access.

Data validation

Dropdowns and rules that keep manually-entered data usable.

Showcase

  • Monthly sales report — pivot-based summary for a small retail team
  • Workforce tracker — shift and attendance log ahead of BI modeling
  • Inventory report — stock levels and reorder flags with data validation
