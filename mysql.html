MySQL & relational data analysis

Most of my analysis starts in SQL — cleaning the data at the source and answering the first round of business questions directly against the tables.

What I use SQL for

Data cleaning

Standardizing types, trimming strings, and flagging nulls before analysis begins.

Joins

Multi-table joins across fact and dimension tables to bring context to raw transactions.

Subqueries

Nested queries to filter or aggregate before joining back to the main result set.

CTEs

Common table expressions to break complex logic into readable, testable steps.

Window functions

Ranking, running totals, and month-over-month comparisons with DENSE_RANK and LAG.

Business questions solved

  • Which regions and sub-categories discount most heavily, and does it actually hurt profit?
  • Which departments are consistently understaffed against required headcount?
  • Which products or customers drive the most loss at high volume?
  • How does profit margin move month over month, and where does it diverge from the trend?
